- Optimized fullscreen and screen rotation logic, and added related configuration settings
- Restored support for watching and searching live streams (NicoNico)
- Tapping anywhere on the video title row now expands/collapses the title details

- Fixed an issue where fullscreen swipe gestures were unreliable and could accidentally minimize the video
- Fixed an issue where search history entries were randomly not saved or moved to the top
- Fixed an issue where bold/italic/strikethrough styling was not correctly applied in comments
- Fixed a crash on startup on certain TVs.
- Fixed an error that occurred when refreshing subscriptions for channels with no videos (YouTube)
- Fixed a blank screen issue during login (NicoNico)
- Fixed an issue where videos failed to play (BiliBili)
